# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Shift primarily serves South African startups, local businesses, and owners whose current websites feel dated or no longer reflect the quality of their work. Visitors are often busy, cost-conscious, and looking for a capable partner who can make the next step feel clear rather than technical or intimidating.

## Product Purpose

Shift helps good local businesses look as ambitious online as they are in real life. The website should explain the studio clearly, show convincing work, and make it easy for a prospective client to start a conversation.

## Positioning

Premium web design and practical digital upgrades for local businesses—without big-agency distance or pricing.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: Start your shift.
- Secondary CTA: Explore the work and learn how Shift approaches a website.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: Make the shift. Get shift done.
- Belief ladder: Shift understands the cost of looking outdated; Shift can produce polished, high-quality work; the process is direct and collaborative; a better website is within reach; starting is easy.
- Proof on hand: two projects—Gouveia Socials and Carli & Co. Final measurable outcomes will be added when supplied.

## Brand Personality

Precise, ambitious, and energising. Shift should feel futuristic and premium without becoming cold, corporate, or inaccessible. Its language can be cheeky in decisive moments, while the design and service details keep the business credible. It should leave a motivated business owner thinking, “This is the upgrade I have been waiting for.”

## Anti-references

Avoid cheap template styling, inflated agency language, generic corporate stock imagery, playful visuals that weaken trust, and technology jargon that makes a local business owner feel excluded. Boldium is a quality reference for confident scale, project storytelling, motion, and professional restraint—not a layout to copy.

## Design Principles

1. Practice what we sell: Shift’s own website must be the strongest proof of its standards.
2. Show before telling: make projects and crafted digital previews the centre of the experience.
3. Make ambition feel achievable: premium should never mean distant or unaffordable.
4. Keep momentum visible: every page should lead naturally toward an easy conversation.
5. Be specific and human: plain language, honest scope, and no empty agency claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard accessibility. Respect reduced-motion preferences, keep essential meaning independent of colour, use visible focus states, and maintain generous touch targets and readable type across mobile and desktop.
